Airbnb rank #4 of 320, with click-through of 56% against 11% for similar listings. Guests are finding it and clicking hard. But the booking rate is 1.8% against a 18% market. This is a conversion problem, not a visibility problem, so the answer is price and terms, not more exposure.

The next 90 days are 16 booked, 72 open and 3 owner-blocked (Labor Day, Sep 4–6). Forward-60 revenue is $10,587 and the market demand index sits soft at 0.7–0.9. The open inventory is heavy and some of it is in-window right now:

  • Last-minute discount is 0%, in a soft, late-booking market where the recommended setting is around 37%. This is the single biggest missing lever.

Turn on last-minute discounting, up to roughly 25 to 30% inside 21 days, with the $200 floor protecting the downside. Relax min-stay to 2 nights on soft midweek shoulder dates. Both moves convert the strong click-through this listing already earns.
