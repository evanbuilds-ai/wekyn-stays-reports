Loon Cabin Market Behind market

Forward revenue is $7,855 over the next 30 days but only $9,408 over the next 90. August and September are contributing almost nothing yet, and the market hit 81% last August. Pickup is healthy (10 nights in the past 30 days) and the base price is right where PriceLabs wants it. The problem is concentrated:

  • Three overrides dated June 21, no reason logged, hold Aug 7 at $560, Aug 8 at $555 and Aug 14 at $575 against algorithm recommendations of $421–444. Those are the two biggest open weekends of a 13%-occupied August.
  • Last-minute discount is 0% — the most conservative possible setting in a month where the market discounts to fill.
  • September books ~58 days out, so its window opens now. Labor Day weekend is open and priced at market ($403–457/night).
The fix

1) Remove the Aug 7 / 8 / 14 overrides so those weekends reprice to the $420–450 algorithm level (confirm intent with Neil first, they're undocumented). 2) Turn on last-minute discounting, up to ~25% inside 21 days. The $130 minimum price guards the floor.
