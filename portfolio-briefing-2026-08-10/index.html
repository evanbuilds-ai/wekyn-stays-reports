Butterfield Market Behind market

Revenue for the next 30, 60 and 90 days is the same number: $9,671. That is the whole story. Nothing at all is booked past August 29, and MPI slides from 1.1 in the next 30 days to 0.7 across 90. Seventy-three of 87 bookable nights are open.

The $200 floor holds the line. 3) Leave the weekend rates alone; they are already under the comp median.

Forward revenue over 90 days is $2,685 and RevPAR for the next 30 is $73. September has not a single booked night. Seventy-seven of 87 bookable nights are open, MPI runs 0.5 to 0.7, and there are zero date overrides on the listing, meaning nobody has touched it manually all season.

Meanwhile first-page competitors run a $418 median while Loon's open September nights sit mostly between $220 and $345. We are already well under the comp set and still not converting.

  • The Airbnb listing is the weak link, not the rate. Three of the four reservations Loon does hold came through VRBO. Guests who see it on VRBO book it; guests who click it on Airbnb do not.
  • Last-minute discount is 0% while the market read suggests 34%, and Aitkin books 1 to 19 days out in August. This is the fastest lever available.
  • Aug 11–13 are blocked. Worth confirming these are intentional and not a leftover hold, since nothing else on the calendar suggests a reason.
The fix

1) Book a full listing audit before touching price again: lead photo, title, first three photos, review count. The funnel says guests click and then walk away. 2) Turn last-minute discounting on at up to 30% inside 14 days to catch the short Aitkin window. 3) Confirm the Aug 11–13 block with the owner. No further rate cuts until the listing audit is done.
